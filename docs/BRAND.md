# Astral Orchestrator brand

## Concept

The mark represents three model lanes converging around Sol, the coordinating
lead. The three paths and central spark should remain one connected symbol.

## Palette

| Name | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Midnight navy | `#0B1020` |
| Solar gold | `#F6C453` |
| Lunar ice blue | `#8ED8F8` |
| Off-white (dark-surface navy replacement) | `#F7F8FB` |

## Asset use

- Use `assets/brand/astral-orchestrator-mark-light.png` on light surfaces.
- Use `assets/brand/astral-orchestrator-mark-dark.png` on dark surfaces.
- Use `assets/brand/astral-orchestrator-app-icon.png` for app or launcher
  contexts that require an opaque square with a midnight navy background.
- Use `assets/brand/astral-orchestrator-favicon-32.png` for standard 32px
  favicons and `assets/brand/astral-orchestrator-favicon-64.png` for
  high-density 64px favicon slots.
- Use `plugins/astral-orchestrator/skills/astral-orchestrator/assets/icon.png`
  for the plugin icon.

The transparent marks are square assets with alpha antialiasing. Keep the full
square and preserve the mark's proportions. Use 32px as the minimum general
icon size; below that, check legibility before shipping. Prefer the 512px or
1024px source when a larger rendering is available.

## Do not alter the mark

Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, crop, or add effects to the mark. Do not
separate the paths or rearrange the paths and central spark. Choose the light
or dark transparent mark for the surface instead of modifying its colors.
